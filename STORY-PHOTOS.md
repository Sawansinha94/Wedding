# Our Story — photo placeholders

Put your photos in the `images/` folder using **these exact names**:

- `story-new-beginning.jpg` — A new beginning (top-right)
- `story-two-hearts.jpg` — Two hearts, one promise (middle-left)
- `story-forever.jpg` — Forever begins here (bottom-right)

When the filenames match, photos appear automatically after refreshing the website. For best results use landscape JPG images (roughly 3:2 ratio). The cards crop with `object-fit: cover` to keep the existing layout. No HTML editing is required.

Until photos are supplied, each card displays an elegant placeholder with its caption.
